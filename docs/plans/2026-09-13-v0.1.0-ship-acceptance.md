so each line can be FALSE, with the evidence that settles it. Per
[Plan Acceptance Criteria](../standards/plan-acceptance-criteria.md).

**Read the status column as the honest current answer, not as a plan.** Most of it is ❌, and that
is the finding. Two criteria are marked ❓ because I do not know the answer and will not guess.

## What the statuses add up to

**Three met.** The claims are honest, the naming is honest, and the findings from the last human
run are closed with evidence. That is real, and it is the work of the last two days.

**Five not met, and none is exotic.** Two red tests, a broken CI leg, an unpatched vulnerability
with an available fix, an unaccepted one without, and 197 strings nobody has read.

**Four unknown, and those are the ones that matter most.** Whether the product does its core job,
whether it builds and publishes at all, and whether it can be rolled back, are unanswered — not
failing, *unasked*. AC-11 is the sharpest: the release act has never been rehearsed.

**The honest summary: v0.1.0 cannot be declared shippable today, and the blocker is not defect
count — it is that the most important criteria have never been tested.**

## Proposed order, if the PO accepts this bar

Cheapest first, and deliberately: AC-11 before AC-9 before AC-10, because a build that does not run
makes every other answer moot, and a browser journey needs something to run against.

1. **AC-11** — attempt a build and publish to a throwaway target. It has never been tried.
2. **AC-4, AC-5** — the two red tests and the MinIO image. Both are small and both are blocking.
3. **AC-9** — the persona journeys against that build.
4. **AC-10** — the PO drives the real journey, as on 2026-09-06.
5. **AC-6, AC-8** — the `datasets` bump, the written acceptance of `rsa`, and the string review.
6. **AC-7** — confirm `govulncheck` runs once the fix merges.
7. **AC-12** — write and exercise the rollback.

## What the PO is being asked

1. **Is this the right bar?** Add, remove or reword any criterion. It is a proposal.
2. **Which are release-blocking and which are not?** My draft treats all twelve as blocking, which
   is almost certainly stricter than intended — AC-12 in particular may belong to a later version.
3. **Who answers AC-10?** It is the only one no agent can settle.

Until those are answered, **v0.1.0 is not shippable and no build should be published.**
